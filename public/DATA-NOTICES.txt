Third-party data shipped with this TypeWords deployment
=======================================================

The TypeWords program is GNU GPL-3.0. The files below are data, not
program source, and this notice does not relicense them.

CET-4 word list
  public/dicts/en/word/CET4_T.json
  public/list/word.json
  public/list/recommend_word.json
  This list (headwords, phonetics, short glosses, example sentences,
  and etymology notes) is the dictionary already published in the
  upstream TypeWords tree. It is loaded locally so typing practice
  works without calling typewords.cc. Treat the text as third-party
  lexical data. Do not assume it is GPL-3.0.

New Concept English lesson text and audio
  public/dicts/en/article/NCE_1.json
  public/sound/article/nce1/*
  Those textbook transcripts and recordings are omitted from the
  daresay.app static bundle. Article practice in the program still
  runs, and you can import your own texts. The files remain in the
  corresponding source tree because they came with the upstream
  project; publishing them again on this site is not part of this
  deployment.

Pronunciation playback
  Pressing the speaker asks the browser to request audio from
  https://dict.youdao.com/dictvoice . That call is optional, happens
  only after you play a word, and is unchanged from upstream. Typing
  practice does not depend on it.

This deployment does not load typewords.cc analytics, Baidu analytics,
or 51.la.
